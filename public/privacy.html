Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

go janey. ("we," "us," or "the app") helps people discover local events in Boulder, Colorado and nearby towns. This policy explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information We Collect

If you create an account, we collect your email address to send you a magic sign-in link. Optionally, you may add a name, handle, bio, and interests to your profile.

We store which events you save or mark as interested, so that information can be shown back to you in the app.

How We Use Information

Your email is used only to authenticate you (sign you in). We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Saved events and profile info are used solely to power app features like your Saved list.

Location

If you allow it, we use your device's location to show how far events are from you and to center the map on your area. Your location is used only on your device — it is never sent to our servers, stored, or shared with anyone.

Event Data

Event listings shown in the app are sourced from public event information (such as Google Events) and manual curation. This event data is not personal information about app users.

Data Storage

Account and app data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database provider. We do not store payment information — go janey. is a free discovery app and does not process purchases.

Your Choices

You can sign out at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at gojaneyboulder@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at gojaneyboulder@gmail.com.
